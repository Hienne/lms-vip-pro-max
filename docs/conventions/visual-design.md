# Visual design: what not to build

The mockup in `lms-mockup/` was generated by an AI tool. Its data, actions, states, behaviour and
the meaning of its copy are the spec; parts of its look are not. This doc lists only what must not
be taken from the mockup and what must not be used anywhere in the UI. Everything else follows the
mockup and `ui-library.md`.

Read it before building or restyling any UI, converting a mockup screen, or writing UI copy. Where
the mockup, a neighbouring file or the `/design-system` page does something listed here, do not
reproduce it, and list the change as a deliberate difference in the pull request.

Items marked **guarded** fail `template-scan.spec.ts`, so `npm run check` catches them. Check the
rest by eye on every changed screen at 1280 and 375px, light and dark.

## Do not take from the mockup

- An eyebrow: a small uppercase label above a title (`ui.eyebrow`, `eyebrow=` on
  `ui-page-header`, section numbers like "01"). **guarded**
- Uppercase or letter-spaced small labels: nav groups, table headers, menu labels, card meta.
  Uppercase is for acronyms and codes only (CIF, EOD, NV-101). **guarded**
- A hero banner or a greeting at the top of a page ("Chào …, hôm nay học gì tiếp nhỉ?", "Chào mừng
  trở lại").
- An icon inside a tinted square or circle: KPI tile, list tile, dialog icon, empty-state circle,
  feature card.
- A feature or benefit grid (icon, title, blurb, repeated), including on the sign-in page.
- A gradient, watermark, blurred or decorative shape, or glass (`backdrop-filter`). **guarded**
- A coloured edge: `border-left` or `border-top` wider than 1px on a toast, tip, card, nav item or
  result. **guarded**
- Placeholder art: a pastel block with a centred icon, an enlarged Lucide icon, decorative circles.
- A centred empty state with an icon circle inside a card or a table.
- Entrance motion on page content: fade or slide on load, `ui.animate(enter)` outside overlays.
  **guarded**
- Hover lifts (`translateY` on hover), parallax, looping decoration.
- A slogan or tagline (above all two clauses joined by an em dash), self-praise chips ("WCAG 2.2
  AA", "Dark mode").
- A demo or mockup note in the product ("Bản mockup v2 · dữ liệu giả lập") outside the dev tools.
- Purple, violet, or any colour copied from a default palette (Tailwind's `#7c3aed`, `#ede9fe`).

## Do not use

### Badges and chips

- A status tone (`success`, `warning`, `danger`, `info`) on a category: role, "Bắt buộc", course
  type, unit. Status tones are for state only.
- A badge for an attribute: staff code, account type, role, unit.
- More than one coloured badge on a row or a card.
- Chips or pills that only list values (permissions, tags). Chips are for toggles and filters only.

### Colour

- `accent` (hoa mai yellow) for anything but an achievement: not navigation, tips, warnings or
  decoration.
- Red for anything but an error, a destructive action, overdue or failed.
- `*-subtle` fills on decorative tiles or covers.
- A status colour for a chart series that does not mean that status.
- More than one primary button per view.

### Type

- More than three font weights on a screen.
- `ui.font(display)` outside h1 and h2, or below `ui.text-size(xl)`.
- `xs` (12px) for anything but timestamps and footnotes.
- Text whose diacritics clip or touch the line above at the smallest size the screen uses.

### Shape and elevation

- `ui.radius(xl)` or a new radius value. **guarded** (`xl`)
- A pill-shaped button, badge or chip; `ui.radius(full)` on anything but avatars, switches,
  progress bars and status dots.
- A shadow on anything that does not float (popovers, menus, dialogs, drawers and toasts may have
  one).
- A card inside a card, or a card around a single value.

### Layout and feedback

- Centred layout, except a single focused form (sign-in) or a whole-page error (403, 404).
- An eyebrow or an icon in `ui-page-header`.
- More than one inline action on a table row, or a destructive action inline.
- Two facts stacked in one admin table cell, or a wrapped admin table row.
- Two feedback messages for one event (two toasts, or a toast and an alert).
- A component that branches on `data-palette`, or a palette that brings back anything on this page.

## Known violations (do not copy)

These are still in the code. Do not copy or extend them. Fix one only when the task is that fix,
then delete its row here and, for a guarded item, its entry in `KNOWN_VIOLATIONS` in
`apps/lms/src/app/template-scan.spec.ts` (the spec fails while a fixed entry is still listed).

| Pattern                | Where                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Glass                  | `layout/header/header.scss` (`backdrop-filter`)                                                                        |
| Badges for attributes  | `features/account/profile` (staff code and account type as outline badges)                                             |
| Two inline row actions | `features/admin-org/users` (edit and lock inline)                                                                      |
| `/design-system` page  | hero (gradient, `xl`, entrance motion), space section (entrance motion), principles grid (icon tiles), chips           |
| Extra radii            | `xl` on the dialog (`libs/ui/src/styles/components/_dialog.scss`), `full` on chips, Cobalt's `control: 999px`          |
| Tokens                 | the `hero-*` colour tokens; Cobalt's gradient hero; Giấy's dot-grid texture                                            |
| Entrance motion token  | the `enter` keyframe moves 8px over `base` (the change to 4px and `fast` is central: do not override it per component) |
| Copy                   | the sign-in slogan and "Chào mừng trở lại"; the always-on mockup note                                                  |
| Chart colours          | `chart` 3 equals `warning`, 4 is Tailwind violet, 5 equals `danger`                                                    |
